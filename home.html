About

I'm Joe Elias, a doctoral student in the Ecology and Evolutionary Biology Department at Binghamton University, where I work in the Grames Lab for Insect Ecology and Conservation. Ultimately I seek to improve and leverage non‑lethal tools to monitor wildlife. Currently my work focuses on using historical bioacoustics data to inform insect conservation and develop GIS and modeling methods for optimized site selection to build large-scale monitoring networks.
