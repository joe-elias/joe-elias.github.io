Teaching Statement

Every person deserves a chance to improve themselves and have access to the resources to accomplish this goal. As researchers and educators, we have a responsibility to ensure those resources are available and to create conditions where people can reach their full potential. This includes being aware of the biases and privileges we bring into our work and how they shape our expectations and decisions. As an educator, I aim to build an environment where students can take intellectual risks, reflect on their progress, and develop mental dexterity in different scenarios and problems. I have taken formal pedagogy training and grad-level pedagogy courses to benifit my teaching skills and material.
